Curio Alchemy is a game where players combine elements and build a collection of discoveries. This policy explains the information stored on the device and the Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform components used to serve ads and manage privacy choices.

## Game data

Game progress, selected language, and audio preferences are stored in the app's local storage on the device. The current release has no game account, developer-operated game server, or analytics service that sends this game data to the developer. You can remove local game saves by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

## Advertising and privacy choices

The app may show optional rewarded ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share the following data with Google services for ad delivery and measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention:

- The device IP address, which may be used to infer general/approximate location.
- App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
- Diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.
- Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other device/account identifiers.

Google states that data transmitted through the Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Actual ad and consent behavior depends on region, the messages configured in the publisher's AdMob account, and the user's choices. The UMP consent form is shown at app launch when required. If a Google privacy message requires users to be able to change their choices later, a **Privacy options** entry appears in Settings.

For more information about advertising data processing, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can also manage advertising ID preferences in your Android device settings.

## Retention and deletion

Game saves remain on the device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled; the developer does not store game accounts or saves on a server. Google retains data processed by its advertising SDK under the terms and privacy policies for the relevant services. This app does not let users create an account.

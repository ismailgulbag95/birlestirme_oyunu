# Identity brief — Curio Alchemy

## Product and audience

An alchemy discovery puzzle built around combining two known elements at a 3D workbench to discover an item and grow a collection. The likely audience is casual puzzle players who enjoy experimentation, collecting, and readable visual feedback.

## Brand character

Curious, warm, considered, discovery-rich. Avoid childish clip-art, occult menace, and generic “magic app” styling.

## Competitive cues to avoid

Avoid a literal flask, cauldron, wand, generic sparkle, four elemental icons arranged as a logo, or a plus/merge arrow. The game already has a detailed workshop scene, so the identity should be a compact sign rather than another miniature scene.

## Word map

Curio / curiosity / reveal / aperture / collection / workshop / ingredients / result / amber light / night blue.

## Concepts explored

1. **Curio aperture (letterform):** an open C-shaped ring points toward a single diamond, making the missing piece feel like the next discovery. Recommended.
2. **Convergent pair (abstract):** two offset forms meet at one clear result. Directly expresses combining, but risks reading as a generic merge icon.
3. **Workshop window (emblem):** a framed diamond nested inside a square window. Ownable as a badge, though less distinctive at small sizes.

## Selection

Use concept A as the primary mark. It connects the Curio name to discovery without depicting game objects. Keep the amber mark on midnight blue for the app icon; use the single-colour mark for small or printed uses.

## Palette and type

- Midnight: `#11162B` — primary background
- Amber: `#E9AB3B` — signature mark and key emphasis
- Warm highlight: `#F4C66C` — focal accent
- Water cyan: `#62C4E8` — supporting gameplay accent
- Fire coral: `#F07955` — secondary gameplay accent, not a core logo colour
- Store headline type: system sans-serif/Arial for reproducible exports; use a licensed geometric sans for production UI if desired.

The mark is custom vector geometry. Store-title typography is live display text, not part of the logo master. No font licensing assumption is needed for the mark.

## Use and limits

Leave clear space at least the width of the central diamond. Use the amber mark on midnight, or black/white on a plain contrasting surface. Do not add glow, bevels, outlines, extra elemental symbols, or perspective. The app icon is a square tile with a safe, centered mark; do not put the name in the icon.

`Curio Alchemy` is a working name recommendation, not a trademark or store-name clearance. Search exact and similar marks in target markets before public use.
